# tntf_wifi-vls-devicefree-dataset_real_world2026
These are Wi-Fi &amp; VLS device-free datasets were obtained by our research team, including Muhammad Wafa, Yasir Abdul Aziz, Faris Afif Ridyasmara, and Muhammad Nur Aslam Anugerah. The experimental work was supervised by Dwi Joko Suroso at the Department of Nuclear Engineering and Engineering Physics, Universitas Gadjah Mada, in 2026.
The datasets are designed to support research on indoor localization, with a particular focus on multi-sensor, device-free approaches applied in real-world environments. Our objective is to develop accurate and efficient device-free location estimation methods that leverage complementary characteristics to improve performance. This work can enable niche applications, such as monitoring the well-being of elderly individuals.

# Dataset overview
The dataset includes Wi-Fi Received Signal Strength Indicator (RSSI) and VLS illuminance data collected at typical classroom and lobby.
